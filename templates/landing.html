FAQ
Is BiteBack only for students?＋

No. BiteBack is for anyone who wants to manage food spending and reduce waste, including students, working adults, roommates and families.

Are meal costs exact supermarket prices?＋

No. Costs are estimates to help with planning. Update them using your actual local prices.

Does an expiry reminder guarantee food is safe?＋

No. Dates are reminders only. Always check storage instructions and food condition, and follow food-safety guidance.
